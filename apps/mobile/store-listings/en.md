## Full Description

**Stop rewatching travel videos trying to remember every place.**

ReelRoutes turns any YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, or X travel video into a structured, interactive map itinerary — in seconds.

**How it works**
Paste a travel video URL → AI extracts every location → pins drop on a map → you have a ready-to-use trip plan.

**Key features**

- 📍 Auto-extract locations from any travel video
- 🗺 Interactive map with all stops pinned
- 📅 Day-by-day itinerary planning with AI
- 🚗 Route optimisation — save hours of driving
- ✅ Mark stops as visited with diary notes
- 💬 AI travel chat for recommendations and tips
- 👥 Share trips and invite travel companions
- 💰 Budget tracking with expense splitting
- 📴 Offline access — no signal needed on the road
- 🌍 Available in English, Spanish, Japanese, Korean, and Portuguese

**For creators and travellers**
Whether you discovered a hidden café in Kyoto or a must-see waterfall in Patagonia — ReelRoutes makes sure you actually find it when you get there.
